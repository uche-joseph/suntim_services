Privacy Policy

At Suntim International Services, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our services.

Information Collection

We collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and passport details when you book a trip or inquire about our services. We may also collect information about your travel preferences, academic interests, and emergency contacts to better serve your needs and ensure your safety while abroad.

Information Usage

The information we collect is used to process your bookings, communicate with you about your travel arrangements, and provide personalized recommendations for study abroad programs. We may also use your data to improve our services, conduct market research, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information Protection

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. These include using encryption for data transmission, secure servers, and regular security audits. We also limit access to your personal information to employees who need it to perform their job functions.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You may also object to or restrict certain types of data processing. To exercise these rights, please contact our privacy officer.

Terms of Service

By using the services of Suntim International Services, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions. Please read these carefully before using our services.

Service Description

Suntim International Services provides travel planning and booking services for students and educators participating in study abroad programs. Our services include flight bookings, accommodation arrangements, program enrollment assistance, and travel insurance coordination.

User Responsibilities

You are responsible for providing accurate and up-to-date information when booking our services. This includes personal details, travel documents, and academic requirements. You must also comply with all local laws and regulations in your home country and the country you are visiting. Users are expected to behave responsibly and respectfully while participating in study abroad programs.

Liability

While we strive to provide accurate and reliable services, Suntim International Services is not liable for any losses, damages, or injuries that may occur during your travel or study abroad experience. This includes but is not limited to flight cancellations, program changes, or personal accidents. We strongly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance to cover potential risks.

Cancellations and Refunds

Cancellation policies vary depending on the specific services booked and the policies of our partners (airlines, educational institutions, etc.). Please refer to the specific terms provided at the time of booking. In general, we will make every effort to process refunds in accordance with these policies, minus any non-refundable fees or charges.
